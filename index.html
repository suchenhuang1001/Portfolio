About

I'm Suchen Huang, a Senior UX Researcher and Strategic Designer with experience spanning enterprise fintech, public sector civic tech, and emerging industrial products.

My work sits at the intersection of user needs and business strategy — helping cross-functional teams build products that are not only usable but genuinely meaningful to the people they serve.

I bring a mixed-methods approach to every project: leveraging behavioral data, ethnographic inquiry, concept testing, and innovation workshops to surface insights that drive measurable impact.

Beyond individual projects, I care about elevating research culture within organizations — building frameworks, upskilling teams, and advocating for equity and inclusion in design.

🏦 Enterprise Fintech
Led research at BMO InvestorLine and financial institutions, shaping navigation, IA, and GenAI strategy for self-directed investors.
🏛️ Civic Technology
Designed and tested inclusive experiences at the NYC government level, advocating for accessibility across diverse communities.
🌾 Emerging Technology
Led 100+ touchpoint research initiatives for next-generation electric autonomous tractor ecosystems with HCD methodologies.
📈 Research Operations
Built research frameworks, upskilled teams on unmoderated testing platforms, and established UX maturity across organizations.
